ABOUT US

One band,
Many styles.

The Kings is a Latin American band featuring Mathias de Oliveira Martins as its songwriter and lead vocalist, alongside Paulo Roberto Cardoso Fernandes as its producer. Blending a variety of musical styles, the band draws primarily from Mathias’s passion for Pop and R&B, complemented by Paulo’s influences from Hip-Hop and Rap. What sets The Kings apart is the importance it places on emotion. For the band, creating beautiful music is only the beginning — true expression comes from feeling every note, every word, and every melody. Because music is not just meant to be heard; it is meant to be felt.
